Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data we collect on this website, why, and what your rights are. We handle your data in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Belgian privacy law.

1. Who is responsible

Melodic Drift is a collaboration between Tanzgemeinschaft and Chrom Recordings. Both are responsible for the data collected on this website. The website is operated by Claudio Capo Comm V, Dijlestraat 19, 2060 Antwerpen, Belgium (company number BE0830.857.854).

For questions about privacy, or to use your rights, write to melodicdrift@tanzgemeinschaft.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Newsletter sign-up: your first name, email address and country, so we can send you news about Melodic Drift from Tanzgemeinschaft and Chrom Recordings. Legal basis: your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time through the link in every email.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message, so we can answer your question. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in replying to your request.
  • Website statistics: with your consent we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited and from what kind of device. Legal basis: your consent.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic request details in server logs to keep the site secure and working. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.

3. Cookies and consent

We use Cookiebot to ask for your consent before any non-essential cookies are set, such as those used for statistics. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the cookie settings of this website. The full list of cookies we use is shown below.

4. Who receives your data

We only share data with the services needed to run the site:

  • MailerLite, which stores and sends our newsletter.
  • Google, for Google Analytics (only with your consent) and Google Fonts, the typefaces used on this site. When fonts are loaded from Google, your IP address is sent to Google.
  • Cookiebot, which manages your cookie choice.
  • Our hosting and email providers, who deliver the website and the messages you send us.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We never sell your data.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Contact messages: up to 12 months after we have dealt with your request.
  • Website statistics: up to 14 months.
  • Server logs: for a short period, as set by our hosting provider.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to limit or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. If we process your data based on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. Send your request to melodicdrift@tanzgemeinschaft.com.

If you feel we have not handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données) at gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

7. Links to other websites

This site links to Instagram, SoundCloud, Spotify and Beatport. We have no control over how those services handle your data. Please read their own privacy policies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new service. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
