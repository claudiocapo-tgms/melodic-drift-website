The night

Music that moves in long arcs

Melodic Drift is where two labels share one room. Tanzgemeinschaft and Chrom Recordings bring the artists they believe in and let the set breathe.

Expect patient arrangements, layered pads over a rolling low end, and selectors who read the floor instead of racing it. No rush, no peak-hour theatrics, just a steady drift from the first record to the last.
