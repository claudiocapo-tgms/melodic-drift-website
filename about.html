About Melodic Drift

Melodic Drift is an electronic music event concept created through the collaboration between Chrom Recordings and Tanzgemeinschaft, two of Belgium's leading electronic music labels: 2 Belgian labels, 1 international vision, united by music. Proudly Belgian. Internationally Connected Driven by Music. Celebrating the deeper, more melodic side of electronic music.

Both labels have established themselves as recognised names within the international electronic music scene, building extensive artist networks, developing their own musical identities and connecting artists and audiences across borders. With a shared passion for quality electronic music and unforgettable dancefloor experiences, both labels combine their experience, international connections and creative vision to create unique events that go beyond the traditional club night.

The story of Melodic Drift goes back to 2022, when Chrom Recordings and Tanzgemeinschaft first joined forces during Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE). Since 2022, The Flying Dutchman Café, located right in the heart of Amsterdam, has become the home of our annual ADE showcases, welcoming artists and audiences for memorable nights of Progressive House and Melodic House & Techno.

Born during ADE, Melodic Drift has established itself as a successful event concept, bringing together two international music communities and attracting music lovers, international artists and industry professionals for nights filled with atmosphere, connection and positive energy. Hosted yearly during ADE, spread over two days (Friday and Saturday), Melodic Drift is now expanding beyond Amsterdam, bringing its distinctive sound and identity, its unique atmosphere to clubs, promoters and event organisers worldwide.
