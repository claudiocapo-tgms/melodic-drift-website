The night

Two labels. One vision.

Melodic Drift brings together the experience, musical vision and international networks of two established Belgian electronic music brands.

Together, they combine respective artist rosters, industry connections and creative vision to deliver a distinctive event experience.

Tanzgemeinschaft
An independent platform connecting music, artists and audiences across digital and live experiences. A magazine for 16 years and a label for 8, based in Antwerp (BE).
Chrom Recordings
Founded in Ibiza in 2017 and now based in Ghent (BE), Chrom Recordings has developed into an international platform for Progressive House, Melodic House & Techno and Indie Dance, working with a diverse roster of established and emerging artists.
Music

The sound

At the heart of Melodic Drift lies a carefully curated musical journey through Progressive House, Melodic House & Techno, and Indie Dance. Expect deep grooves, hypnotic rhythms, atmospheric textures, emotional melodies and powerful melodic moments. From warm-up sessions that gradually build the atmosphere to peak-time performances that take the dancefloor to another level, every event is designed to create a natural musical flow. The focus is always on the music, the journey and the connection between the artists and the crowd.
