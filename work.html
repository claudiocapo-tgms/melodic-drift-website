Work with us

More than just a party

Melodic Drift is more than a label showcase: it's a flexible event concept, tailored to your venue and audience. With the international networks of Chrom Recordings and Tanzgemeinschaft, we take care of everything needed to bring it to life.

What we offer

Take the journey further

Concept & artistic direction

From the initial creative concept to the musical identity and overall atmosphere of the event.

Artist bookings & line-up curation

Access to the international artist networks of Chrom Recordings and Tanzgemeinschaft, complemented by carefully selected guest artists, local residents and DJs of your choice.

Branding & visual identity

A recognisable event identity, professional artwork, promotional visuals and event-specific communication materials.

Marketing & promotion

Social media campaigns, artist promotion, digital communication and coordinated promotional activities through our own label channels and international networks.

Event production & management

From planning and coordination to artist management and on-site event organisation. We can take care of the complete production, allowing you to focus on your venue and audience. Whether you need a complete event production or simply want to bring the Melodic Drift identity and musical experience to your venue, we offer a flexible approach to make it happen.

Your venue

Your venue. Our concept.

Every location has its own identity, audience and atmosphere. That's why no two Melodic Drift editions need to be exactly the same.

We offer different event formats, ranging from intimate underground club nights to full-scale label showcases, festival editions and special events.

Our concept can include:

  • Full Chrom Recordings x Tanzgemeinschaft label showcases.
  • Tailor-made international DJ line-ups.
  • Collaborations with local promoters and resident DJs.
  • Special guest appearances and exclusive B2B performances.
  • Complete branded events and full event productions.
  • If within driving range, we can bring equipment (sound & light) and screens for a visual setup.
